# Owner Handbook

The Owner sets goals, priorities, permissions, and risk tolerance. AI handles
routine engineering choices, implementation, repair, tests, and evidence
without asking the Owner to select files, commands, or technical fixes.

Ask the Owner when a choice changes product direction, removes major behavior,
creates an external commitment, performs an irreversible action, sends private
content to a new service, or accepts material security, privacy, data-loss, or
cost risk. Record durable project-specific decisions in project instructions or
Project Context.

Optional capabilities such as persistent context, external research, codebase
maps, automation, and cross-project data should be adopted only when useful.
Their provider, data boundary, retention, cost, artifacts, and update policy
belong to the consuming project.

At completion, the useful questions are simple:

1. What changed?
2. What was actually verified?
3. What remains uncertain or unverified?
4. Is an Owner decision still needed?

Implementation, review, verification, and acceptance are separate claims. If
one host performs several, it says so rather than implying independence.
